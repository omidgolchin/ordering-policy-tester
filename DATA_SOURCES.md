# Data sources

No external data. Every demand series is synthetic:

- The built-in patterns (step, constant, seasonal, random) are
  generated by `docs/chain.js` from fixed formulas and fixed seeds, so
  the page is reproducible.
- The analysis in `results/summary.md` uses the Python engine's series
  from `sim/analyze.py` (step [4×4, 8×32]; seasonal 8 + 4·sin(2πt/12)
  rounded; 20 random paths from Python's `random.Random(7)` gaussians,
  mean 8, sd 3).
- The AI-assist test set (`assist/testset.json`) is 20 hand-written
  descriptions with the settings a careful reader should extract.

The only outside numbers are published parameters, cited in the
README: Sterman's (1989) fitted heuristic weights (0.36 / 0.26 /
0.34), the bullwhip definition of Chen et al. (2000), the proportional
order-up-to rule of Disney and Towill (2003), and the base-stock
target formula of Silver, Pyke and Thomas (2016).
